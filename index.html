About us

Multi-Disciplinary Professional Land Surveyors

Mohlatlole and Associates is a 100% black-owned, multi-disciplinary corporation with extensive expertise in cadastral, engineering, topographical, and sectional title surveys. We are well-represented across South Africa, with our primary offices located in the Limpopo Province.

Our Mission: To provide empowerment opportunities through effective project implementation, while uplifting previously disadvantaged individuals and communities through practical and formal training in Professional Land Surveying.
